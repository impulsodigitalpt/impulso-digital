PRIVACY INFORMATION

Your data, clearly explained.

This information explains how data sent in an enquiry to Rubira Digital is handled and how the website selects a language. The form does not subscribe you to a newsletter or complete a purchase.

Questions about your data

For questions or requests about your data, contact Rubira Digital using the contact form.

Data and purposes

The form asks for your name, email and project description. Your business name and preferred plan are optional. It also records the selected option (one-off project or ongoing support) and the form language. If the enquiry comes from an advert, available source, medium, campaign, term and creative values (UTM parameters) are included to identify the campaign. The page URL is also included without query parameters or its fragment. We use the data only to assess the enquiry, reply and prepare a proposal. Do not include identity documents, passwords or sensitive information.

Basis and use

The data is processed to respond to your enquiry and, where applicable, take steps at your request before entering into a contract. When you contact us on behalf of a business, contact details are processed in the legitimate interest of responding to a commercial enquiry initiated by that person. The form checkbox confirms that you have read this information; it does not authorise marketing unrelated to the enquiry.

Providers and transmission

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you submit the form, your browser sends the fields directly to FormSubmit, which forwards them to the enquiry inbox. See the FormSubmit privacy information. The service documentation states that submissions archived on its platform are retained for 30 days. The hosting and form providers may process technical data necessary for the operation and security of their services, according to their policies.

Automatic language selection

When you open a Portuguese page without a saved language choice, your browser may request an approximate country from Country.is based on your IP address. The service receives your IP as part of that connection. We send no form fields, campaign parameters or page URL to it, and request no precise location. We use only the country code: Portugal keeps Portuguese; other countries select English. Rubira Digital does not store your IP or add the country to your enquiry. The country code is cached only for the browser tab session. The provider states that it does not log requests; see its service information.

If the country lookup fails, we use your browser’s preferred language: Portuguese selects Portuguese; other languages select English. You can always choose PT or EN. A manual choice is stored locally in your browser until you clear website data and takes priority over automatic selection. Direct English URLs remain available. IP location can be inaccurate, for example when using a VPN.

Retention

If an enquiry does not lead to a contract, its inbox data will be deleted or anonymised within 12 months of the last interaction about it. If you purchase a service, data needed to perform the contract and tax documents follow their own statutory retention periods, which may differ. The FormSubmit archive follows the period stated above.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing and, where provided by law, object to processing or request portability. Send your request using the contact form. You may also complain to Portugal’s National Data Protection Commission.

Advertising and browser storage

This website does not use advertising cookies, Google Analytics or Meta Pixel. Only the five UTM parameters listed above are associated with an enquiry when the form is submitted. Other campaign parameters and click identifiers are not included in the data sent to FormSubmit. Browser storage is used only for your language choice and the session country code described above. The hosting platform receives the requested address.
